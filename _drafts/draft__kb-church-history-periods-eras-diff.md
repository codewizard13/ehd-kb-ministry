Excellent and very insight‑driven question — and one that theologians and prophetic leaders actually debate quite a bit.

The **Prophetic \& Apostolic Reformation (1990s–Present)** and the **Emerging Kingdom Era (Now → Future)** overlap in time, but they describe **two distinct movements in function and focus**:

***

### 🕊️ **Prophetic \& Apostolic Reformation (c. 1990s – 2020s)**

**Focus:** Restoring *church government* and *spiritual authority* through modern apostles and prophets.

**Core ideas:**

- Re‑discovery of the **five‑fold ministry** (Eph 4:11 — apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors, teachers).
- Emphasis on **prophecy, deliverance, spiritual warfare, and revival culture.**
- Formation of **apostolic networks** (not denominations) for spiritual covering and accountability.
- Great leaders: C. Peter Wagner, Bill Hamon, John Wimber, Cindy Jacobs, Che Ahn, Dutch Sheets, etc.
- Worship and prophetic creativity explode (IHOP, Bethel, MorningStar).
- The movement’s heartbeat: *“Reform the Church to operate as in the New Testament—under apostolic and prophetic direction.”*

👉 **In short:** This era restores heaven’s *governmental structure* to the Church so she can function biblically and supernaturally again.

***

### 🌅 **Emerging Kingdom Era (2020s → Future)**

**Focus:** Extending **kingdom influence beyond the Church** into society — the maturing fruit of the previous reformation.

**Core ideas:**

- The Church, now equipped and mature, shifts from revival gatherings to **cultural transformation.**
- Emphasis on **the seven mountains** (government, business, education, media, arts, family, religion).
- Apostolic leaders function as **kingdom architects** rather than only church ministers.
- Prophetic vision expands to national awakening, justice, and reformation of systems.
- Language shifts from *revival* to *reformation* and *occupying until He comes.*

👉 **In short:** This era moves from *restoring structure* within the Church (the NAR focus) to *releasing influence* of the Church into the world systems.

***
